description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Pati Sauce[/b] - a spicy vinegar condiment na pang-timpla sa kahit ano. Goes great on rice, stir fry, soup, you name it.
description=[*] [b]Sinigang Seasoning[/b] - tamarind mix na pang-asim. Combine it with a pot of water to start your Sinigang broth, then throw in veggies, meat, and other seasonings and cook it up. Sarap-sarap ‘to, promise.
description=[*] [b]Bagoong Alamang[/b] - shrimp paste. Amoy malakas, sarap mas malakas pa. Not for the faint of nose, but worth it.
description=[*] [b]Pan de Sal[/b] - the OG breakfast bread. Bake your own dough into fresh, fluffy rolls para sa almusal ni Lola.
